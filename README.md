# Pixora Studio

Pixora Studio brings photo editing, video editing and PDF-to-image conversion together in one desktop creative workspace.

## Photo Editor

- **Layer-based editing** — add, organise, duplicate, hide, lock and remove layers, with individual opacity and positioning controls.
- **Canvas tools** — create a blank canvas, change canvas dimensions and open existing images.
- **Layer transforms** — move, resize, rotate and flip image or shape layers, with optional snapping and aspect-ratio controls.
- **Cropping and cutouts** — crop layers and isolate parts of an image using shape selection, magic cutout or a brush-based selection tool. Refine selections with tolerance, softness, contiguous selection and invert options.
- **Image resizing** — resize image layers independently from the overall canvas.
- **Image adjustments** — tune brightness, contrast, saturation, exposure, highlights, shadows, temperature and tint.
- **Filters** — apply grayscale and sepia treatments.
- **Text and shapes** — add editable text and shape layers, with controls for typography, alignment, fill, stroke and appearance.
- **Drawing** — paint directly onto the canvas with an adjustable brush size and colour.
- **Editing workflow** — preview the original image, zoom and fit the canvas, and use undo and redo.
- **Image export** — save edited images with format-specific export options.

## Video Editor

- **Video playback and preview** — open and preview video files in the editor.
- **Playback controls** — seek through a video, view its current time and duration, and adjust or mute audio.
- **Trim timeline** — select a section of a video using the timeline handles and preview the selected duration.
- **Trimmed video export** — export the selected section and monitor export progress.

## PDF Converter

- **Page previews and selection** — view a PDF's pages, inspect page dimensions, and choose individual pages or select all/none.
- **PNG and JPG output** — convert selected PDF pages into image files.
- **Render resolution** — choose 72, 150, 300 or 600 DPI and see the resulting pixel dimensions.
- **JPEG quality** — control JPG output quality independently of lossless PNG output.
- **Batch conversion** — export multiple selected pages with progress reporting and cancellation.
