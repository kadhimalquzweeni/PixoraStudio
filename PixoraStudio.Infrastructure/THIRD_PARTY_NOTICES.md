## Real-ESRGAN x4plus model

Pixora Studio can download the Real-ESRGAN x4plus ONNX model when the user chooses AI Upscale. The model is downloaded from the pinned revision of [anakhiu/realesrgan-onnx](https://huggingface.co/anakhiu/realesrgan-onnx/tree/b699e714820b6b277cfc92be0c3c8b522d47bc9b), an ONNX export of the official [xinntao/Real-ESRGAN](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN) x4plus weights.
